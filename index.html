Built with vanilla JavaScript and the Canvas API. No libraries, no server, and every network is trained live in your browser.

How it works

  1. Sense

    Each car casts 7 rays from −90° to +90°. Each ray measures how close the nearest wall is. Those 7 distances plus the car's speed are the 8 inputs to its brain.

  2. Think

    The brain is a small feed-forward neural network (8 → 10 → 6 → 2, tanh). It outputs a steering value and a throttle value every frame. Nobody programs how to drive. The weights start out random.

  3. Score

    A car dies when it hits a wall or makes no progress. Its fitness is the number of checkpoints it passed, plus a bonus for average pace and for finishing 3 laps.

  4. Evolve

    When every car has stopped, a genetic algorithm breeds the next generation. The top 3 brains survive unchanged (elitism). The other children come from tournament selection, uniform crossover of two parents' weights and Gaussian mutation.

Things to try

  • Train on Oval, then switch to Serpentine. Good brains generalise to tracks they've never seen.
  • Draw your own track with ✎ Draw.
  • Push the mutation rate very high or very low and watch evolution stall.
  • Once the AI completes laps, hit Race the AI and try to beat it.
